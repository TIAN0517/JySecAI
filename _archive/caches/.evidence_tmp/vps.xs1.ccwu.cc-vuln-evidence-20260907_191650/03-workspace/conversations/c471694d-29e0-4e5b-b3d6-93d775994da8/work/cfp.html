The Standard of Excellence

CERTIFIED FINANCIAL PLANNER® certification is the standard for financial planning. CFP® professionals meet rigorous education, training and ethical standards, and commit to CFP Board to serve their clients’ best interests.

A Career in Financial Planning

CFP® certification means being subject to a higher standard in delivering financial advice.

CFP® certification provides a roadmap for advisors to help their clients across all areas of their financial well-being.

Katherine Medina, CFP®

When you earn CFP® certification, you’ll be able to help people and have the chance to give back to your community.

Lauryn Williams, CFP®

CFP® certification allows me to express my commitment to the financial planning profession.

Matt Aaron, CFP®

CFP® certification gives my clients greater confidence that I am subject to a high standard of care.
