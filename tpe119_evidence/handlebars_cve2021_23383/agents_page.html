        The University of Sydney logo consists of two elements:

        • the shield
        • the University of Sydney wordmark.

        The logo should be seen as a complete unit, with the shield and word-mark always appearing in partnership. The mono logo will be used in all applications.

        To obtain a copy of the logo, contact one of our regional managers. Use of the University logo requires approval from the Brand team, please share your artwork with your contact and allow several days for approval.

        • Have we requested a document? Upload it via the Sydney Student Portal, rather than email - this will reduce the turnaround time. The system notifies us when a document has been uploaded. 
        • You can also reduce the turnaround time by including an applicant's passport or photo ID when submitting a new application.
        • Students can apply for credit (advanced standing) for subjects they have previously completed at another institution if they are a direct equivalent to units of study at the University. Advise students to read through the course handbook for their chosen bachelor's degree and intended major(s), to see how credit could potentially fit into the study plan.
